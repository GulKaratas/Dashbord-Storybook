## Usage Examples

### Backgrounds
- `bg-white` - Ana arka plan
- `bg-secondary-50` - Hafif gri arka plan
- `bg-primary-500` - Birincil arka plan

### Text Colors
- `text-secondary-900` - Ana metin
- `text-secondary-600` - Yardımcı metin
- `text-primary-600` - Vurgulu metin

### Border Colors
- `border-secondary-200` - Varsayılan border
- `border-primary-300` - Vurgulu border
